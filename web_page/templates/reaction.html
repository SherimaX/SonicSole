Instructions

This activity tests your auditory reaction time using foot pressure. Reaction time is the time it takes for your brain to detect a stimulus, in this case an auditory stimulus (a sound), and send a signal to your muscles to respond.

Lift your foot with the SonicSole off the ground. Then, click the "Start Reaction Test" button. After a random delay, you will hear a sound. As soon as you hear it, step down with your foot as quickly as possible. The force sensors will detect when you apply pressure.

If you step before the sound, the trial is considered invalid and you will need to start the activity again.

The system calculates your reaction time by measuring the time between the sound and when the pressure on the foot sensor crosses a defined threshold. This value reflects how quickly your nervous system processes the sound and initiates movement.

Reaction time is influenced by several factors, including attention, fatigue, and even how well your brain communicates with your muscles. After each test, your measured reaction time will be displayed on the screen in seconds.
