Explanation

You likely noticed balancing was easier with your eyes open. That’s because the brain relies on visual cues to keep your body’s center of mass over your foot. When vision is removed, your body must rely more on muscle feedback to maintain balance. Both systems work together to stabilize posture and prevent falls.
