Instructions

Press "Start Walking" to begin. Walk forward on your forefeet—this means keeping your heels completely off the ground. Your goal is to maintain this position for 15 seconds and walk as far as possible. If your heels touch down at any point, the trial ends and counts as a failure. Force sensors continuously monitor heel pressure. As soon as heel contact is detected, a signal is sent to the computer to stop the timer and log your time. This activity trains balance, ankle stability, and calf strength, all of which are key in activities like sprinting, dance, and gymnastics.
