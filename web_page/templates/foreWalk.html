Instructions

Press "Start Walking" to begin. Walk forward on your forefeet—keep your heels completely off the ground. Maintain this for 15 seconds and walk as far as possible. If your heels touch, the trial ends and counts as a failure. Force sensors monitor heel pressure and will stop the timer if contact is detected.
