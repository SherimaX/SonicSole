Setup Guide

Materials

  • The hardware assembly, 3D-printed case, logic unit, and two foam sheets
  • A pair of insoles sized for the user

Prepare the Case

  1. Insert the logic unit into the 3D-printed case and close it. Place the battery at the bottom of the case, the cable connector at the top, and the speaker in the lower indent.
  2. Place both insoles face down on a foam sheet and trace their outlines with a marker.
  3. Cut the foam along the outlines you traced.

Attach the Hardware

  1. Attach the hardware assembly to the insole for the dominant foot. Remove the backing paper from both pressure sensors and place them on the marked heel and forefoot locations. Fold the large white wire along the insole, tape it down, and route the connector out near the heel. Secure any loose wires or hardware with tape before moving on.

Finish the Insole

  1. Glue the foam onto both insoles with tacky glue and let it dry for 5 to 10 minutes. On the instrumented insole, keep the glue on the edges so the wiring is not compressed.
  2. Plug the large white cable from the instrumented insole into the logic unit through the slot in the 3D-printed case.

Wear the System

  1. Place both insoles inside the shoes, then clip the case securely to the shoe.
