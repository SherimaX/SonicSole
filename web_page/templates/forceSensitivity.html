Instructions

This activity tests your ability to match a target pressure level.

Step 1: Calibration – Apply your maximum force. This sets your 100% force.
Step 2: Match the randomly chosen target % of your max. Force is measured in real-time.
Step 3: Your accuracy is calculated based on how close your applied force is to the target.

This activity tests proprioception – your sense of body position and effort – to control pressure precisely.
